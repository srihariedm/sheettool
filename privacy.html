Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information SheetTool ("the app") accesses from your Google Account, how it's used, and the choices you have. SheetTool is a personal tool its developer uses to manage their own Google Ads accounts.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and allow access, SheetTool can only access what you approve on Google's consent screen. For Google Ads, that includes:

  • Google Ads account details, such as account names and IDs
  • Campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords and budgets
  • Performance reports, such as impressions, clicks, views and cost

The app never sees or stores your Google password.

How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the app's features: showing reports and creating or updating campaigns, ads and budgets in the Google Ads accounts you've authorised. It isn't used for any other purpose, isn't sold, and isn't used to build profiles of anyone.

Sharing

SheetTool doesn't sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. Data is exchanged only with Google, through Google's official APIs. The only exception would be a disclosure required by law.

Storage and security

Sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored privately and are never published or shared. Report data is kept only as long as it's needed to manage the accounts, then deleted.

Your choices

  • You can remove SheetTool's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. After that, the app can no longer access your data.
  • You can ask for any stored data about you to be deleted by contacting the developer (see below).

Google API Services User Data Policy

SheetTool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact the developer through this site's GitHub repository, or at the support email shown on SheetTool's Google sign-in screen.
