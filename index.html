SheetTool

A private, personal-use tool for managing Google Ads accounts through Google's official APIs.

What it does

SheetTool connects to Google APIs, such as the Google Ads API, with the account owner's permission. It's used to:

  • View campaign performance and reports
  • Create and update campaigns, ads and budgets

It only works with Google Ads accounts that the signed-in Google account is allowed to manage.

Who it's for

SheetTool is used by its developer to manage their own advertising accounts. It isn't a public product, and there's no sign-up.

Signing in with Google

SheetTool uses Google's standard sign-in and consent screen. Google shows exactly what the app is asking for before you allow it, and the app never sees your Google password.

You can remove SheetTool's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Your data
How SheetTool handles Google user data is explained in the Privacy Policy. The rules for using the app are in the Terms of Service.
